# W3-01 — Ba gợi ý công thức theo tồn kho

## Hợp đồng

| Test | Input | Expected Output | Mutation bắt |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `recommendationsOrderUrgentReadyQuick` | 4 recipe, 1 ingredient estimated 1 day, fully cookable dish, quick dish | exactly 3 distinct recipes: ingredient needing priority, cookable dish, then <20 minute dish | return fixed catalog order / ignore urgency/readiness/duration |
| `urgentUsesOnlyCurrentItemsWithinEstimateWindow` | have=false, future estimate, overdue estimate | only currently present item at 0..2 days affects urgent score | count missing, overdue, or later items as urgent |
| `recommendationsFillWhenCategoryMissing` | small/partial catalog | stable distinct list, at most 3 and no fabricated recipe | duplicate fallback or unstable ordering |
| `RecipeBundle` | JSON bundle with ids, minutes, steps and slots | parsed recipes are rendered from the bundled data at runtime | hardcode recipe cards in Compose |
| Partial ready / 20 minute boundary / duplicate ids | one required slot missing, duration exactly 20, duplicate bundle id | partial recipe is not marked ready; 20 is not quick; ids remain unique | use any slot / inclusive threshold / duplicate result |
| `alternativeIngredientsInOneSlotCountAsOneUrgentUse` | one slot with two urgent alternatives vs two separate urgent slots | two separate usable slots rank higher | count every alternative as a consumed ingredient |

## Biên

- Priority uses inventory marked present and estimate `daysLeft` in `0..2`; overdue food is not described as safe or as a priority to eat.
- The second suggestion must satisfy all required ingredient slots.
- The quick suggestion has `minutes < 20`.
- Tie order follows the JSON bundle order. Suggestions contain no duplicate recipe IDs.

## Implementation and verification

Đã triển khai gợi ý theo thứ tự ưu tiên, sẵn nguyên liệu, rồi thời gian nấu; không tạo bản ghi giả, loại ID trùng và giữ thứ tự catalog khi đồng hạng. Mỗi required slot đóng góp tối đa một điểm nguyên liệu ưu tiên dù có nhiều alternative. Cùng một alternative ưu tiên 0–2 ngày được dùng nhất quán ở nhãn, scoring và hành động trừ sau khi nấu.
