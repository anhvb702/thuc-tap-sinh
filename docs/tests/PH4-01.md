# PH4-01 — Random fallback and contextual notice

## Contract

- **Goal:** Keep random recipe selection within the caller-provided (diet-filtered) list, prefer cookable recipes, then prefer the lowest number of missing required slots. Daily reminders may suggest a diet-safe recipe when no ingredient needs attention, at most once every three days.
- **Inputs:** Recipe list, pantry state, excluded recipe id and seeded `Random`; for notices, pantry, ingredient catalog, current date, last daily send, diet-filtered recipe inputs and last contextual nudge date.
- **Expected:** Random selection returns null for empty/excluded pools; a cookable candidate wins; otherwise only candidates with the minimum missing-slot count are eligible. Notice fallback names a recipe, marks itself contextual, respects the diet restriction, and is suppressed when fewer than three days have elapsed.
- **Edges:** Empty recipe list, all candidates excluded, alternative ingredients in a slot, no expiring items, Sunday shopping notice, same/future `lastSent`, nudge dates in the future.

## Tests and mutations

1. `fallbackPrefersRecipesWithFewestMissingRequiredSlots` — one pantry item and candidates needing one versus two more slots; only the one-missing recipe is returned. Catches reversing the priority and choosing from all candidates.
2. `emptyWeekdayListSuggestsDietSafeRecipeAndThrottlesForThreeDays` — vegetarian restriction filters beef, copy includes the remaining recipe, day +2 is suppressed and day +3 is allowed. Catches skipping diet filtering, changing the threshold, and returning no fallback.
3. Existing `prefersCookableRecipes`, `neverReturnsExcludedId`, and `returnsNullForEmptyList` retain the cookable, exclusion, and empty-pool contracts.

Mutation verification for all six required variants has not yet been recorded.
